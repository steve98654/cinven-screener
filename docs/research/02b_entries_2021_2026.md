## Conclusion

**Count:** 39 platform entries announced in the window (37 strictly inside it, plus Compre and Miller, announced Nov to Dec 2020 and closed in 2021, kept as flagged boundary cases). 17 form the non-outlier core; 22 are outliers (SFF or SF2 funds, US-led, minority, fund-to-fund, club or boundary deals). 10 add-ons by portfolio companies are listed separately and excluded.

**Core entry profile (17 flagship, European-led, control deals):**

- EV: median ~€2.0B, p25 ~€1.4B, p75 ~€2.75B, on the 11 core deals with a sourced EV (think-cell ~€1.0B, RBI >€1.0B, True Potential ~€2.1B, Alter Domus €4.9B, idealista €2.9B, Vitamin Well ~€3.0B, Grant Thornton UK ~€1.8B, Artefact ~€1.15B, Smart Communications ~€1.6B, UAX ~€2.0B, Theramex ~€2.6B). The six core deals without a published EV (Arcaplanet, Netceed, MBCC, Amara NZero, Domia, Nutrisens) are mostly mid-sized, so the true core median is likely nearer ~€1.5B (low confidence). Only 3 of the 11 EVs are Cinven-stated (idealista, RBI, plus Ufinet and Envu in the outlier set); the rest are Bloomberg, Sky News, CFNEWS or NKP figures.
- Equity cheque: almost never published. Two derived estimates (Theramex ~€1.1B, UAX ~€1.0B, EV less reported debt, low confidence) plus Policy Expert and Alter Domus structures imply flagship cheques of ~€0.7B to ~€1.5B; SF2 cheques are an order of magnitude smaller (Flint EV ~€0.22B).
- Revenue at entry: median ~€510M, p25 ~€210M, p75 ~€880M (n=8: think-cell €88M, True Potential ~€215M, Theramex ~€430M, Arcaplanet ~€593M, GT UK ~€854M, MBCC ~€945M, Netceed >€1,000M, Artefact ~€200M).
- EBITDA at entry: median ~€82M, p25 ~€80M, p75 ~€150M (n=5: Artefact ~€60M, think-cell ~€80M EBIT proxy, Smart Communications ~€82M derived, Vitamin Well ~€150M, True Potential ~€157M). Implied multiples where both sides exist: 13x (True Potential), 16x to 20x (Artefact, Flint, Smart Communications, Vitamin Well).
- Headcount at entry: median ~1,350, p25 ~500, p75 ~2,750 (n=12).

**Deal-type mix (core):** secondary buyout 12 (71%), primary from founders or a partnership 4 (24%: think-cell, RBI, Grant Thornton UK, plus Objectway, Flint, Ongoing in SF2 outliers), corporate carve-out 1 (6%: MBCC). Across all 39: secondary 22 (56%), primary 10 (26%), carve-out 5 (13%), minority 2 (5%). Sponsor-to-sponsor is the dominant route; founders usually roll over a significant minority.

**Sector mix (core):** Business Services 6 (35%), TMT 3, Consumer 3, Financial Services 2, Healthcare 2, Industrials 1. Across all 39, Financial Services leads with 11 (28%) because the SFF and SF2 funds concentrate there; TMT 8, Business Services 8, Consumer 5, Healthcare 4, Industrials 3.

**Country mix (core HQ):** Spain 4, UK 4, France 4, Germany 2, Italy 1, Luxembourg 1, Sweden 1. Across all 39: UK 9 (23%), US 7 (18%), Spain 5, France 5, Germany 4, Sweden 4, Italy 2, Switzerland, Isle of Man, Luxembourg 1 each. Lead office: London 15, Frankfurt 6, New York 6, Madrid 5, Paris 5, Milan 2.

**Founder or family involvement at entry:** 12 of 17 core deals (71%) had a founder or family reinvesting or remaining in management; across all 39 it is 20 (51%), 18 no, 1 unknown (Salsify). Every SF2 deal is founder-partnered.

**Funds:** Seventh 12, Eighth 17 (verified by regulator filings for Domia and Nutrisens, inferred from vintage for the others), SFF 6 (Miller, Compre, IFGL, Säkra, Finaxy, Policy Expert, all named by Cinven), SF2 4 (Objectway, Flint, Ongoing, Optio, named in the Sep 2026 SF2 close release).

**How 2024 to 2026 differs from 2021 to 2023:**

- Pace: 19 entries in 2021 to 2023 against 20 in 2024 to Nov 2026, but the 2021 cohort alone had 11 (post-Covid Fund 7 deployment), 2022 had 6 and 2023 only 3; 2024 had 8, 2025 had 7 and 2026 so far 5.
- Size moved up: core EV median ~€1.0B (2021 to 2023, n=3 with data) against ~€2.3B (2024 to 2026, n=8, p25 ~€1.7B, p75 ~€2.9B), driven by Fund 8 (€13.2B) buying Alter Domus, idealista, Vitamin Well, Theramex and UAX.
- Deal type shifted to sponsor exits: 2024 to 2026 core is 90% secondary buyout (9 of 10) with one primary (Grant Thornton UK); 2021 to 2023 core was 43% primary (think-cell, RBI, True Potential) and 43% secondary.
- US exposure fell: 5 US-headquartered platforms in 2021 to 2023 (Drake, BioAgilytix, Nitel, Envu, Archer) against 2 in 2024 to 2026 (Regenity, Salsify), and Nitel was sold to Comcast in 2025.
- A new mid-market channel opened: SF2 (closed Sep 2026 at €2.3B, co-led by Luigi Sbrozzi and Michael Weber) made four founder-partnered control deals in 13 months (Objectway, Flint, Ongoing, Optio) at EVs around €0.2B to ~€0.5B, the first sub-€500M Cinven platforms since the SFF broker deals.
- Sector tilt: 2024 to 2026 core is 40% Business Services (Alter Domus, Domia, GT UK, Artefact) and brings Healthcare back (Nutrisens, Theramex) after a 2021 to 2023 run of Consumer, Industrials carve-outs and US software.
- Founder involvement eased slightly (86% of 2021 to 2023 core against 60% of 2024 to 2026 core) but remains the norm, including founders rolling into sponsor exits (idealista, Vitamin Well, Nutrisens, Artefact, UAX).

**Status checks on names the brief listed as exited:** Miller (sold to GIC, Mar 2024), Nitel (Comcast, Apr 2025), Arcaplanet (Fressnapf, Jul 2024) and Jaggaer, Kurt Geiger, Viridium, Planasa, Premium Credit, Tractel, Envirotainer, Chryso, STADA, MasOrange, NewDay book, Synlab stake (pre-2021 platforms) are confirmed exits. think-cell, Arxada, Säkra and Taxwell (Drake) remain on cinven.com's current portfolio as of 9 Nov 2026: think-cell is in a reported ~€3B sale process (Bloomberg, Arma Partners mandate, early 2026), Arxada went through a 2025 to 2026 recapitalisation and amend-and-extend (Octus, Debtwire), and no Säkra or Taxwell exit was found. Restaurant Brands Europe sale to a founder-led consortium was agreed 6 Nov 2026. Netceed appears on the realised list without any exit release found (open question). 'Group of Butchers' is an Equistone then Parcom asset with no Cinven link in any source found; treat as not a Cinven deal.
