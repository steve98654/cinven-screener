## Conclusion

**Typical Cinven platform entry, 2015 to 2020:** a EUR ~0.8B to 1.2B enterprise value secondary buyout of a European market leader with ~1,000 to 2,000 employees, bought with a majority stake from another sponsor, led from London, in Business Services, Consumer or Healthcare. Cinven announced 30 platform investments in the window (Fifth Fund tail, the whole Sixth Fund, first Seventh Fund deals and the first two Strategic Financials Fund deals). 18 form the non-outlier core; 12 are outliers (two SFF deals, two insurance balance-sheet platforms, three US-headquartered deals, one minority stake, one fund-to-fund continuation, three deals at or above EUR 5B EV in club structures).

**Core EV (EUR M, published figures only):** median 900, p25 497 to p75 1,215, range 338 to 3,420 (n=14 of 18). Published EVs cover 14 of 18 core deals; the four unpublished (Chryso, PPF, One.com, Barentz) are estimated in the table with method and confidence.

**Core EV including the four ~estimates:** median 740, p25 481 to p75 1,191 (n=18). Smallest core deal Kurt Geiger (EUR ~338M), largest LGC (EUR ~3.4B); 13 of 18 sit between EUR 350M and EUR 1.3B.

**Core equity (EUR M):** not published for any core deal. Equity values are only public for the two take-privates, both outliers: STADA EUR 4.1B (50:50 with Bain) and MasMovil EUR 2.96B (three-way). Indicative core equity from reported leverage: NewDay GBP 500M debt on ~GBP 1B (equity ~GBP 500M shared with CVC); Chryso EUR 270M TLB on an EV of ~EUR 500M (equity ~EUR 230M); INSEEC U ~7x leverage, ~EUR 430M debt on EUR 800M (equity ~EUR 370M). Cinven's own floor is an equity ticket from EUR 200M (Seventh Fund, unquote May 2019). Working assumption: core equity cheques of EUR ~200M to 600M, confidence medium.

**Core revenue at entry (EUR M):** median 435, p25 240 to p75 730, range 60 to 1,000 (n=10 of 18).

**Core EBITDA at entry (EUR M):** median 69, p25 58 to p75 101, range 50 to 171 (n=7 of 18). Implied EV/EBITDA where both are published: Labco ~9x, Hotelbeds ~17x, Kurt Geiger ~8x (Reuters), JLA ~13x (floor), INSEEC U ~13x, LGC ~20x (Reuters source), Envirotainer marketed at ~20x.

**Core headcount at entry:** median 1,130, p25 800 to p75 2,080, range 270 to 7,000 (n=17 of 18).

**Deal-type mix (core):** secondary buyout 14 (78%), corporate carve-out 2 (11%), primary (founder or family) 2 (11%). Sponsor-to-sponsor secondaries dominate (14 of 18, sellers include BC Partners, GTCR, Sycamore, Advent, Varde, LBO France, Pamplona, Hg, AAC, Accel-KKR, Apax France, KKR). The two core corporate carve-outs are Hotelbeds (TUI) and Allegro (Naspers); the other carve-outs in the window (ERGO Italia, TK Elevator, Miller) are outliers. Primary deals: Planasa (family) and Barentz (family); One.com kept its founder CEO but the seller was Accel-KKR, so it is classed as a secondary.

**Sector mix (core):** Business Services 6 (33%), Consumer 4 (22%), Healthcare 3 (17%), Financial Services 2 (11%), Industrials 2 (11%), TMT 1 (6%).

**Country mix (core):** UK 5 (28%), France 3 (17%), Spain 3 (17%), Germany 1 (6%), Luxembourg 1 (6%), Poland 1 (6%), Hungary 1 (6%), Sweden 1 (6%), Denmark 1 (6%), Netherlands 1 (6%). All 18 core deals are European; the three US deals (Bioclinica, Jaggaer, NSM) are outliers.

**Lead office (core, inferred from the Cinven deal team quoted in each PR):** London 12 (67%), Paris 3 (17%), Madrid 2 (11%), Frankfurt 1 (6%).

**Founder or family involved at entry (core):** 3 of 18 (17%): Planasa (owner CEO Darbonne kept a significant stake), Barentz (family-owned, primary), One.com (founder CEO Jacob Jensen continued). RTB House (founders, minority) is an outlier.

**Stake (core):** majority 14 (78%), co-control 4 (22%). Co-control cases: Hotelbeds (CPPIB), NewDay (CVC), Allegro (Permira, Mid Europa), LGC (Astorg, ADIA).

**Fund mix (all 30 entries):** Sixth 17 (57%), Fifth 8 (27%), Seventh 3 (10%), SFF 2 (7%). Fund attribution from Cinven press releases: Fifth Fund investments numbered 9 (Ufinet, Jun 2014), 10 (NPS), 11 (Premium Credit), 12 (Labco), 13 (Tractel; Labco and Synlab counted together), 14 (ERGO Italia), 16 (Tinsa); Fifth Fund made 17 investments in total, so Kurt Geiger is 15th and Hotelbeds 17th. Sixth Fund: Chryso 4th, JLA 9th, Envirotainer 10th; Jaggaer confirmed Sixth (Cinven 2024 release); Barentz 'last' Sixth Fund deal (unquote); LGC first Seventh Fund deal (PE Insights); TKE and MasMovil Seventh (unquote). SFF: Miller and Compre confirmed by Cinven's SFF close release (Jul 2022, EUR 1.5B).

**Pace:** 30 platform entries in six years, five a year; by year: 2015: 6, 2016: 5, 2017: 3, 2018: 6, 2019: 6, 2020: 4.

**FX rates used (approximate annual averages, 1 unit of foreign currency in EUR):** 2015: GBP 1.38, USD 0.9; 2016: GBP 1.22, USD 0.9, PLN 0.229; 2017: GBP 1.14, USD 0.885, NOK 0.107; 2018: GBP 1.13, USD 0.847, SEK 0.0975; 2019: GBP 1.14, USD 0.893; 2020: GBP 1.125, USD 0.876.

**Method:** Roster built from the cinven.com WP REST archive of every post Jun 2014 to Dec 2021 (raw/02a/wpjson), cross-checked against cinven.com portfolio pages (raw/02a/companies). Each entry's PR gives date, seller, headcount and sometimes EV or revenue; third-party EVs from unquote, CFNews, Reuters (via Yahoo and Inquirer), Bloomberg (via PYMNTS and Insurance Journal), PE Insights, PEI/Buyouts, Iberian Lawyer (Mergermarket), Real Deals. Where no EV is published a ~ estimate is given with method and confidence and excluded from the 'sourced only' statistics. FX at approximate annual averages listed in meta.fx_rates_used.
