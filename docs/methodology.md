# Cinven Screener: methodology note

One page. Built 9 November 2026 on public data only: Companies House basic data, the PSC register, filed accounts (iXBRL bulk data and the document API), company websites, public job boards, cinven.com and press.

**What it does:** ranks UK-headquartered companies that a European large-cap buyout fund could acquire, classified into Cinven's six sectors from their websites, sized from their filed accounts against the two bands Cinven's own entries occupy (the flagship Eighth Cinven Fund and Strategic Fund 2), read for ownership route on the PSC register (founder or family, sponsor-owned with hold years, widely held PLC, corporate parent), scored on a ten-dimension rubric adapted from Cinven's published investment approach and 2024 to 2026 deal rationales, and shown with dated why-now signals. Every number carries its source and every rubric sub-score a verbatim quote.

**Register:** Companies House basic company data (snapshot 1 November 2026, 5.71M companies, 5.18M active) filtered to Active companies whose last accounts were FULL, GROUP or MEDIUM (112k): small, micro, dormant and abridged filers cannot be at the fund's scale. Guarantee companies, CICs, overseas branches and the SIC codes for banks, insurers' carriers, pension funds, public bodies and households are removed (92k). Size comes from twelve months of the iXBRL accounts bulk product (29,436 of these companies filed iXBRL) and, for the rest, from the Companies House document API: most large UK groups still file PDF accounts and Companies House rasterises them, so the P&L page is located from the contents page and read by a vision model (Claude Haiku), with the figures flagged on every card. The universe keeps companies with 150 or more staff or £50M or more turnover on the latest accounts, plus FULL and GROUP filers whose accounts are not yet read (size unverified, never ranked).

**Ownership route:** The PSC register (snapshot 9 November 2026, 16.1M rows) is read for each company and walked up to seven levels through UK holding companies, the depth a sponsor's Topco, Midco, Bidco and Newco chain reaches. The route is the top of the chain: individuals holding 50% or more are a founder or family (75% high confidence; 25 to 50% with nobody else on the register is founder-led, low confidence); a corporate PSC on a list of ~300 sponsors, fund tokens, sovereign wealth and pension funds is sponsor-owned, with the hold measured from the sponsor's PSC notification date; an offshore vehicle with a Topco-style name is a sponsor whose name the register does not show; a foreign operating parent is a carve-out candidate; a UK top company that declares nobody above 25% is a UK group or, when the company itself is a PLC, a widely held PLC and a take-private route; employee trusts, public bodies and mutuals are set aside. Subsidiaries and acquisition vehicles collapse onto the highest UK company that files group accounts, so one business is ranked once on its consolidated figures. Known gaps: the register shows 25% bands only; investors below 25% are invisible; sponsors behind Jersey, Guernsey or Luxembourg vehicles are named only when the vehicle carries the sponsor's name.

**Sector:** Domain found by Seznam search with a title match (Bing and DuckDuckGo block this egress) or by a name probe on .co.uk and .com, verified on the page; for companies at scale that the search missed, a model (Claude Sonnet) proposes the site from the legal name and subsidiaries, and the proposal is kept only when the legal name, a subsidiary's trading name or the company number appears on the fetched pages; up to five pages fetched with robots.txt respected. One LLM call at temperature 0 with a fixed taxonomy of Cinven's six sectors plus other, a business-model label, an operating-business flag and a UK-headquarters flag; the evidence quote must appear verbatim in the fetched text or the label is flagged and its confidence set to low. Holding pages, property and infrastructure vehicles, funds and clubs go to other.

**Size bands:** Cinven's measured entries (docs/research/02a and 02b, docs/cv_pattern.json): 35 flagship platform entries 2015 to 2026 with the outliers removed have EV median EUR 1.2B (p25 740M, p75 2.3B), EBITDA median EUR 76M (p25 61M, p75 145M) and headcount median 1,130; the eight 2024 onwards entries have EV median EUR 2.3B. The UK flagship operating deals entered at turnover £100M to £250M and EBITDA £20M to £75M (Kurt Geiger, JLA, True Potential, Northgate Public Services; docs/research/03). Strategic Fund 2 (EUR 2.3B, closed September 2026) takes control positions in founder-owned European mid-market businesses in Financial Services, Business Services and TMT at EV ~EUR 100M to 500M. The screener therefore labels every company by EBITDA proxy (operating profit plus depreciation and amortisation where disclosed): Strategic Fund band £8M to £30M, flagship band £30M to £300M, mega-cap above (TK Elevator and STADA territory, excluded from the calibration as club deals). Turnover is the fallback when no profit line is on file.

**Investment Quality (0 to 100):** Ten dimensions scored 1 to 5 from website text plus structured facts from the filings and the register, each with a verbatim quote, URL and confidence. Two are rule-scored from the accounts: cash generation and margin quality from the operating margin, growth momentum from turnover growth against the prior period. The other eight read the site: market leadership and scale, structural growth trends, resilience and revenue visibility, buy and build potential, internationalisation potential, pricing power and commercial levers, transformation headroom (digital, data and AI), management, governance and ESG readiness. Weights are ours: 15% each for leadership, structural trends, resilience and growth; 10% each for cash generation and buy and build; 5% for the rest. A dimension the model could not evidence is left out of the weighted mean and two or more unscored dimensions grey the score. Adapted from cinven.com's Investment Approach page and the attributes that recur across fourteen 2024 to 2026 deal announcements (docs/research/01, Rubric candidates); the pages describe the firm's approach and deal rationales, and the site says so.

**Entry Fit (0 to 100):** Deterministic and never blended with Investment Quality: size 30 (EBITDA proxy inside either band, turnover as the fallback), route 25 (founder or family 25, sponsor 22 rising with hold years from two to four, offshore vehicle 16, PLC and foreign parent 14, UK group 12), growth 15, margin 10, sector 10 (gate), data quality 10 (accounts verified, website name-matched, label and route confidence). The ranked list sorts on Investment Quality; Entry Fit gates it at 40 and breaks ties. Gates: six sectors, operating business, group head (and the largest company under its immediate parent), founder, sponsor or PLC route (employee trusts and Cinven's own portfolio never rank; a PLC read from a PDF annual report never ranks), size verified at scale (EBITDA £8M or more, or turnover £60M or more with no profit line), website found and name-matched, no manual exclusion.

**Why now (unscored):** Dated facts with their source on every company page: sponsor hold years from the PSC notification date, oldest owner's age from the register's year of birth, shared surnames, founders' rollover stakes alongside a sponsor, new and ceased PSCs in the last twelve months, a holding company incorporated in the last eighteen months at the top of the chain, turnover growth or decline, thin margins, dividends against profit, bank borrowings against EBITDA, negative cash from operations, overdue accounts, name changes, outstanding charges, senior roles open. Approach, caution or context; never scored.

**Leadership:** Current directors and officers for every company in the six sectors come from the Companies House officers endpoint (appointment date, year of birth, nationality, stated occupation). The Leadership Team tab shows board size, tenure, ages, which directors are also owners of record, and the rubric's governance read. The register carries no job titles, so roles need confirming on the company website.

**Validation:** Back-tests on Cinven's 20 UK platforms 2010 to 2026 (docs/backtests.md): 11 fall after the PSC register began and could be re-run on the register as it stood the month before announcement; 8 sat on a route the screener acts on (True Potential founder-led, JLA and Compre and Flint sponsor-owned, LGC and Policy Expert foreign parent, Grant Thornton UK group), and the three misses show the limits (Smart Communications' sponsor appears as individual partners; NewDay and Miller sat behind chains the register did not resolve at the time). Today the screener reads Cinven as owner of True Potential, Smart Communications, Policy Expert and Objectway's UK entity. Pre-deal size from the research sits inside the bands for every clean operating platform. Labels and rubric sub-scores are double-scored on a sample by a second model, and the top 30 are hand-reviewed with exclusions recorded in overrides.yaml with reasons.

**Sources, all free:** Companies House bulk data, REST and document APIs (Open Government Licence); company websites; public job boards; cinven.com; press. LLM calls through the Anthropic API (Claude Haiku 4.5 and 5.5). Not used: LinkedIn, paid databases, CRM.

**Extending:** one ingest adapter per register (Handelsregister, Infogreffe, Registro Mercantil, Bolagsverket) adds a country; the pipeline is unchanged.
